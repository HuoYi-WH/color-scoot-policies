Who operates the game

STARGO LIMITED is registered in Hong Kong and operates the Android game identified by the package com.hy.game.fourcolor. You can reach us at huoyan688@gmail.com.

Information we use and why

  • Saved progress. The game stores your current level, coins, items, reward claims and settings on your device so you can continue playing. Clearing Android app storage can remove that progress.
  • Game activity. The game reports launches, whether you have opened it before, tutorial completion and level activity. A level event can include the level number, how the attempt started or ended, time played and progress made. These measurements help us understand where players progress, retry or leave.
  • Rewarded ads. When you choose a video reward, the game sends the level and the location of the ad button, such as the win screen or an item prompt. Ad services can record requests, impressions, interactions and completion. The game uses the completion result to grant the reward.
  • Device and connection details. Depending on the services in use, providers may receive an IP address, approximate region derived from it, device and operating-system details, app version and installation or session identifiers. They may use the Android advertising ID when available and permitted to serve and measure ads, attribute installs and detect invalid traffic.
  • Diagnostics. Crash and error reports may include stack traces, app state, app version and device details. These help diagnose failures in the game and its integrations.

Who receives information

The game uses the FunSmith publishing SDK to connect to analytics and advertising services. Providers receive information according to the features enabled, the ad network serving a request and the privacy choices that apply.

  • ThinkingData processes gameplay events, including level activity and rewarded-ad events. Adjust can process install and campaign information for attribution.
  • AppLovin MAX mediates ads. Participating networks, including Google AdMob, can receive ad requests and device or advertising identifiers to deliver and measure ads. Google User Messaging Platform supports privacy messages and consent choices where configured. AppLovin privacy policy · Google privacy policy
  • Google Firebase provides app analytics and crash reporting through Analytics and Crashlytics when those services are enabled. Firebase privacy information

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the security of our services. Providers may process information outside the country where you live. We apply safeguards required by the law governing those transfers.

Your choices and rights

Watching a rewarded video is optional. On the win screen you can take the base reward without watching one. Some advertising and measurement services initialize when the game starts. Where a privacy prompt is available, you can use it to set your preferences. Android also provides advertising controls in device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. To make a request, name the game and briefly describe what you need in an email to huoyan688@gmail.com.

We may ask for information needed to verify your request and find the relevant records. We will explain applicable limits and respond within the time required by law. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Children and young players

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you are a minor where you live, review this policy with a parent or guardian and obtain their permission where required. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game can contact us for review.

Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the game, measure activity, resolve support requests and meet legal obligations. Retention depends on the type of record, its purpose and the service handling it. We use reasonable safeguards and delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

Clearing app storage affects the copy on your device. For records already sent to a service provider, contact us using the email above. We work with the relevant provider on requests, subject to legal retention requirements and backup cycles.

This website and email

These pages use static HTML and CSS. GitHub Pages hosts them and records visitors’ IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection · GitHub Privacy Statement

When you email us, we use your address, message and any attachments to respond. Please keep personal identifiers and details about children out of public GitHub posts. Use email for individual privacy requests.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date appears at the top. We will provide any notice or seek any consent required for material changes.
